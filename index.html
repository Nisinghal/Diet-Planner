Health Tips
Thyroid, hair health & wellness guidelines
💪
Workout NutritionGym
  • Pre-workout (60 min): banana + 4 almonds or sattu drink — quick energy
  • Post-workout (30 min): paneer 50g or moong cheela — muscle repair
  • Gym days 1,500 kcal; rest days 1,300 kcal — calorie cycling works
  • Protein 80–90g spread across all meals, not one sitting
  • Strength 3–4×/wk + 7,000 steps daily — optimal toning combo
  • Post-dinner haldi milk reduces cortisol from training
🌿
Eat More — ThyroidThyroidHashi
  • Selenium: sunflower/pumpkin seeds — T4→T3 conversion
  • Zinc: moong dal, chickpeas — thyroid hormone synthesis
  • Anti-inflammatory: haldi daily ✓, walnut, ginger, flaxseed
  • Gut-health: curd, chanch, kadhi — reduces autoimmune activity
  • Iron + B12: ragi, spinach, legumes — fight fatigue
  • Magnesium: almonds, leafy greens — reduces cramps & fatigue
  • Vitamin D: 15 min morning sun + doctor-guided supplement
🚫
Avoid for Thyroid
  • Raw gobhi, broccoli — always cook; destroys goitrogens
  • Soy in excess — blocks hormone uptake; space 4 hr from meds
  • Gluten (Hashimoto's) — your plan uses gluten-free bread ✓
  • Refined sugar, maida — spikes cortisol, worsens inflammation
  • Tea/coffee within 60 min of thyroid medication
  • Alcohol — impairs T4→T3 conversion, disrupts sleep
💇
Hair GrowthHair
  • Iron critical — low ferritin = shedding; dal + spinach + ragi daily
  • Vit C with iron — lemon with dal boosts absorption 3×
  • Biotin: almonds, sweet potato — keratin structure
  • Silica: oats, cucumber — strengthens hair shaft
  • Collagen: amla, lemon, chickpeas — natural collagen
  • 10 glasses water — dehydration stresses follicles directly
  • Coconut/brahmi oil massage 2×/week; avoid SLS shampoos
⚡
Daily Habits
  • 5 min walk after every meal — blood sugar + metabolism
  • Morning drink first (methi water / chia / haldi pani) on empty stomach
  • Curd with every meal — probiotics reduce autoimmune activity
  • Zeera tea after lunch/dinner — reduces bloating ✓
  • Never skip meals — thyroid slows metabolism further
  • 7–8 hrs sleep — leptin and thyroid hormones reset overnight
🔄
Seasonal Swaps
  • Blueberries → Jamun (Jun–Aug) or Anar — same antioxidants
  • Avocado → Banana + walnuts — similar fat profile
  • Strawberry → Amla or Guava — 3× more Vit C
  • Greek yoghurt → Hung curd — same protein, homemade
  • Kale → Palak or Methi — equal iron, year-round
  • Quinoa → Ragi or Foxtail millet — same protein, local
🧪
Digestion & Stomach AcidThyroid
  • Chew 20–30× per bite — triggers stomach acid production
  • Lemon water 10 min before meals — stimulates HCl
  • Low acid = poor iodine/selenium/zinc absorption → impairs T4→T3
  • Signs: bloating, heartburn, persistent iron/B12 deficiency
  • Prebiotic foods daily: garlic, onions, methi, bhindi, tinda
  • Avoid processed foods — deplete stomach acid over time
🧠
Mind–Gut–ThyroidHashi
  • Stress → cortisol → shuts digestion → leaky gut → flares
  • Gut makes 90% of serotonin — gut health = mood health
  • 5–10 min deep breathing before meals restores digestion
  • Avoid overexercising — HIIT spikes cortisol; walks + yoga preferred
  • No screens 30 min pre-bed — blue light raises overnight cortisol
  • Limit caffeine after noon — disrupts gut bacteria balance
📋
Common Myths — Know the Facts
  • Myth: TSH test alone is enough. Insist on free T3, free T4, TPO/TgAb antibodies for full picture.
  • Myth: Iodine supplements help. Excess iodine worsens Hashimoto's — get it only from food (salt, dairy).
  • Myth: Only causes weight gain. Also hair loss, depression, cold sensitivity, constipation, skin issues.
  • Myth: Medication forever. Many reduce dose by fixing root causes under doctor's guidance.
  • Myth: Diet alone cures it. Diet is one pillar; stress, sleep, gut, movement equally essential.
